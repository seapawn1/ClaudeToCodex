On 2026-09-13 the PO emphasized that the SM must independently verify the Sprint Backlog acceptance criteria: these determine whether work can be accepted and presented as a completed Increment at Review.

- Check every selected acceptance criterion and the applicable DoD against the final candidate. Record the candidate version, evidence, result and remaining gaps. Use explicit passed, partial, not tested or failed states; do not substitute task completion, Developer reports or passing fixture counts for acceptance.
- Developers retain ownership of implementation, quality and their Sprint Backlog work plan. The SM verifies the evidence and makes the technical acceptance recommendation; the PO judges value and makes their acceptance and release decisions.
- Operator-led end-to-end verification of the installed candidate is distinct from additional PO testing. Do not require the PO to replay a complete technical test matrix already supported by sufficient evidence. Reuse applicable evidence and propose focused real use only where needed.
- The PO asked the SM to propose the participation arrangement when the candidate is ready. Operators prepare the environment, sessions and evidence; PO participation should focus on ordinary collaboration and value, plus host actions that actually require them.
- Reading reports or hearing the SM's account is evidence review. Do not record it as firsthand experience when the current DoD requires firsthand use. Discussing evidence-only acceptance does not itself change the DoD.
- Acceptance determines what is Done; it does not justify postponing Sprint Review indefinitely. Review still inspects progress, gaps and adaptations according to the Sprint cadence, with incomplete work clearly identified.
