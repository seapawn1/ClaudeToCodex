On 2026-09-14, during Sprint 03 Planning, the PO corrected a one-way arrangement in which Developers drafted the HOW, the SM reviewed it, and the PO only inspected the result at the end.

The PO's standing rule for user-story evidence and the triggers for Empathize, Ideate and prototype testing is maintained in [[sprint-discovery-working-agreement]]. The PO explicitly requested that the rule be stored in memory.

- Include the PO while key experience choices, difficult assumptions, research needs and Ideate opportunities are still open. A relatively simple Sprint still benefits from this participation.
- Conduct the three-way conversation across the PO's Codex and Developer sessions. The PO may speak in either place; the SM and Developer actively bridge relevant views, disagreements and decisions. Do not require the PO to choose an agenda from a menu before discussion can begin or to relay messages between the agents.
- Developers provide facts, uncertainties, options, tradeoffs and a basic HOW. The SM facilitates discussion and challenges assumptions; the PO contributes ideas and decides value and experience tradeoffs. Developer ownership of technical implementation remains intact.
- Distinguish unknown facts that need investigation or a small experiment from a narrow solution space that needs Ideate. Choose the research question and learning target with the PO; do not silently preselect all research routes or present only a converged plan.
- Treat early HOW drafts as shared discussion material. Acceptance supplements receive Developer adversarial review, and Planning closes after the team's discussion and PO inspection resolve the important choices sufficiently to start.
- After Planning, Developers lead execution and adapt the plan. Avoid repeated approval for routine implementation choices; involve the PO when learning changes value, scope, acceptance commitments or material cost, and for prototype feedback and end-to-end acceptance.

On 2026-09-18, the PO specified the Planning sequence for Linux parity. Respect explicit stage handoffs rather than treating document preparation as permission to advance:

- The PO leads the first phase: define the Sprint Goal, extract applicable existing DoD, and select/add/modify PBIs. The SM prepares and records discussion material at the PO's request.
- On the PO's handoff, the SM facilitates PBI refinement, using IDEO methods when they address an actual question. Developer review and discussion follow; important decisions remain with the PO.
- After that discussion, Developers lead the worktree and HOW phase and submit it to the SM for review. Recording the Goal or PBI candidates does not close Planning or authorize implementation.
- For this Linux Planning, the PO explicitly chose to relay messages while the bridge cannot operate. Honor that temporary arrangement and label forwarded reports by source; a written handoff is not evidence of direct delivery. Normal direct agent communication remains the intended product experience.
