On 2026-09-14, the PO requested that the team make the following practices a standing rule. The PO explicitly directed that this rule live in project memory, not in a separate Scrum document.

- At every Sprint Planning, inspect the selected user stories and their real usage context. Establish who encounters the problem, when it occurs, what they currently do, and what outcome matters. Reference user accounts, observations or prior Review/test evidence, distinguishing facts from interpretations and assumptions. A user-story template alone is not evidence; supplement missing context through Empathize before choosing a solution.
- When the Developer finds a direction difficult to solve, or the SM/PO identifies a significant difficulty, unresolved alternatives or inadequate reasoning, bring the problem into a three-way Ideate discussion. Clarify the question, open up different approaches, then compare their assumptions and tradeoffs. Do not leave the Developer to silently absorb the difficulty.
- When a hard or consequential assumption about feasibility or experience remains unverified, use a small, rough, disposable prototype or experiment. State the question and observations needed; involve the PO in actual use when testing experience. Record results, limitations and the resulting decision.
- Enter the mode that addresses the actual gap. Reuse relevant evidence and revisit earlier modes when needed; do not mechanically repeat every mode for every PBI. Difficulty, uncertainty and the cost of being wrong guide the depth of exploration.
- Feed findings back into user stories, AC, HOW and delivery forecasts. Prototype results support learning and route selection; they do not replace acceptance of the final installed candidate or change the DoD.
- Apply the same rule to newly discovered difficulties during a Sprint. Any team member may raise the need; the SM facilitates and removes impediments, Developers own technical HOW, and the PO participates in experience and value choices. Routine implementation decisions remain with Developers.
